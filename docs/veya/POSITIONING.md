# Product positioning

**Now:** Veya is for **Girl Scout families only** — Daisy through Ambassador badge tracking, notebook, and journal.

**Later:** Expand to more people and kinds of real-world learning (not Girl Scouts only), without abandoning the Girl Scout experience.

## How we build for that

- Keep GS content in a remotely switchable pack (`data/gsusa`, seed transform).
- Prefer original Veya summaries and artwork; never hard-code affiliation claims.
- Marketing and FAQ should say “Girl Scouts now” clearly; future expansion is honest “coming later,” not a current promise of multi-program features.
- Do not market homeschool / worldschool / generic youth programs as live use cases until they ship.
